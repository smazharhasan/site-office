What Buildiary does

Every part of the site, in one app

Buildiary brings the store, the money, the schedule and the people of a construction site into one app, on the phone of everyone who works there.

Stores and deliveries

Goods are received against a purchase order, with a photo of the delivery note and the receiver. Every bag that leaves the store goes to a named mistri.

  • Receive against the purchase order, with the supplier's delivery note
  • Release to work and returns, each against a person
  • Stock, low-stock alerts and every movement of an item
  • Find an item by taking its photo

Payments and budget

Supplier bills, advances and payments with proof. The budget by phase, what is committed, and a cash-flow forecast for the whole project.

  • Quotations locked as final before an order is raised
  • Pay only what is due, with a receipt or transfer screenshot
  • Budget by phase: design, grey structure, finishing
  • Supplier account statements to send on WhatsApp

Build progress

Each stage shows planned against built. Site staff report with photos and the owner's side verifies. AI compares before and after photos.

  • Stages with planned dates and percentage built
  • Photo reports from site, verified by the owner's side
  • Inspections, snags and a daily site diary
  • Behind-plan stages flagged on the dashboard

People and site cards

Printed site cards with QR codes, a face check against the registered photo, and attendance with a team photo and the phone's location.

  • Register workers, mistris, group heads and supervisors
  • Sign in with the site card and a PIN
  • Face check when material, cash or goods change hands
  • Days worked and wages by contractor

Water and petty cash

Log every water tanker with its TDS reading, read tank levels from a photo, and keep petty cash with bills and approvals.

  • Tankers with number plate, size and water quality
  • Daily tank levels and water used
  • Petty cash given, spent and returned, by person
  • Every expense with a bill photo and the owner's check

Ask the project

Ask in plain words: are we on schedule, what do I owe the builder next, which materials need ordering. Answers come from your live records.

  • Questions in English, Urdu or Arabic
  • Answers from the schedule, payments, stock and diary
  • Weekly progress summaries for the owner
  • Money is shown only to the owner and the accountant

And underneath

Buildiary is built on ERPNext, the open-source business system used by thousands of companies, so your accounts and stock follow proper rules.

  • Real double-entry accounts behind every payment
  • Every change recorded with who made it and when
  • The full office system on a computer when you need it
  • Your data can be exported whenever you ask
