Questions people ask

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our account in Pakistan or Saudi Arabia, or in US dollars. Send the receipt from Plan & billing in your workspace; we activate your plan once we confirm it.

Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yes. The yearly price is ten times the monthly price, so two months are free.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move from Owner to Builder at any time; the new plan starts when we confirm your payment.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your workspace stays open for a few days after the plan ends, then pauses. Your data is kept, and the workspace opens again as soon as you pay.

Do you send a receipt?

Yes. We email a receipt for every payment we confirm.
