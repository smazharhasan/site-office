What Buildiary does

Everything that happens on site, recorded where it happens

Your supervisor and store keeper use Buildiary on their phones. You see the result on yours.

Stores and deliveries

Goods are received against a purchase order, with a photo of the delivery note and the receiver. Every bag that leaves the store goes to a named mistri.

Payments and budget

Supplier bills, advances and payments with proof. The budget by phase, what is committed, and a cash-flow forecast for the whole project.

Build progress

Each stage shows planned against built. Site staff report with photos and the owner's side verifies. AI compares before and after photos.

People and site cards

Printed site cards with QR codes, a face check against the registered photo, and attendance with a team photo and the phone's location.

Water and petty cash

Log every water tanker with its TDS reading, read tank levels from a photo, and keep petty cash with bills and approvals.

Ask the project

Ask in plain words: are we on schedule, what do I owe the builder next, which materials need ordering. Answers come from your live records.

Questions people ask

What is Buildiary?

Buildiary is a construction site management app for people building their own home and for builders running client sites. It records deliveries, the site store, payments, the budget, build progress and the site team on the phone, in English, Urdu and Arabic. It is made by SMH Global Services.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Buildiary runs in the phone's browser: open your workspace address and add it to the home screen. An Android app is available for site staff.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Choose a plan and pay by bank transfer. We activate it as soon as we confirm the payment. If you don't pay, the workspace pauses after a few days and your data is kept.

Can my builder and site staff use it?

Yes. Each person gets their own sign-in, or a site card with a PIN, and sees only what their role needs. Money stays with the owner and the accountant.

Which languages does Buildiary support?

English, Urdu and Arabic. Each person chooses their own language, and Urdu and Arabic read right to left.

Is my data safe?

Every workspace is a separate database, connections are encrypted, and backups run every day. You can ask us for a full export at any time.

Can I use it for more than one project?

The Owner plan covers two projects and the Builder plan up to ten sites. Contact us if you need more.
